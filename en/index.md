# Tempo Support

Welcome to the Tempo support page.

Tempo is an interval training timer designed to help you organize and manage your workouts.

## About Tempo

Tempo lets you create and customize your own interval training programs.

Organize your workouts with preparation, exercise and recovery periods, arranged into repetitions and cycles.

The app is designed to help you focus on your training without unnecessary settings.
