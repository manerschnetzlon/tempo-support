# Privacy Policy

<small>Last updated: November 27, 2026</small>

## 1. Introduction

Tempo is an interval training timer app independently developed and published.

Your privacy matters. Tempo is designed to work without a user account and without transmitting your data to a server.

## 2. Data used by the app

Tempo allows you to create and manage custom workout programs.

Information entered in the app, including program names, durations, repetitions, cycles, and sound and vibration preferences, is stored locally on your device.

This information is not transmitted to the developer or to third parties by the app.

## 3. Data collection and sharing

Tempo does not collect or transmit personal data.

The app does not include analytics, advertising, or user-tracking tools. It does not require a user account and does not communicate with an external server to operate.

No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties by Tempo.

## 4. Data retention and deletion

Workout programs are stored locally on your device, within the app's storage.

You can delete your programs directly in Tempo. You can also remove the app's locally stored data by deleting the app from your device.

No copy of these programs is stored on a server operated by the developer.

## 5. Third-party services

Tempo uses native iOS features to operate, including sound playback and haptic feedback (vibrations).

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## 6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Tempo's features or privacy practices change. The last updated date will be revised accordingly.
